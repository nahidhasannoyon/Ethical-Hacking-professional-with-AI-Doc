# OSINT Fundamentals

**What is OSINT?**

OSINT (Open Source Intelligence) refers to the collection and analysis of publicly available information to gather intelligence for various purposes, including cybersecurity, investigations, and competitive analysis. OSINT uses data from open sources like websites, social media, forums, and public records to identify valuable insights while adhering to ethical and legal guidelines.

#### Key Platforms for OSINT Research

[**OSINT Framework**](https://osintframework.com/)

* A web-based directory of OSINT tools and resources.
* Categorized by use cases such as username searches, email lookups, domain investigations, and more.
* Provides direct links to tools, making it easy for researchers to access relevant resources.
* Ideal for both beginners and professionals in intelligence gathering.

[**LOPSEC OSINT**](https://www.lopseg.com.br/osint)

* A Brazilian platform dedicated to OSINT tools and techniques.
* Offers localized and global OSINT resources for investigations and information gathering.
* Includes tutorials and articles tailored for the Latin American region.
* Focuses on simplifying OSINT research with a curated set of tools and guides.

#### Summary

OSINT is an essential practice for gathering actionable intelligence from open sources. Platforms like OSINT Framework and LOPSEC OSINT provide structured, easy-to-navigate tools and resources to enhance the efficiency and effectiveness of intelligence operations.
